# 📋 My Planner

A planner built for tracking: work, appointments, study deadlines, family, and content creation, all in one place.


**[Open the live planner →](gloriaokojie.github.io/my-planner)** 

---

## What it does

- **Flexible scheduling** — add any activity with its own date, time, location, and notes. No fixed weekday grid, so it works with rotating shift patterns.
- **Calendar view** — see a full month at a glance, with colour-coded dots for what's booked. Tap any day for details.
- **Custom categories** — rename, add, or delete activity categories to match how you actually organise your time.
- **Countdown Clock** — track multiple upcoming dates (exams, events, deadlines) with live day counts.
- **Content Idea Bank** — a running list of content ideas tagged by platform, plus a batch-day checklist to work through them.
- **Repeat weekly** — set a recurring activity (like a standing study slot) once, and it fills in for however many weeks you choose.
- **Works offline, installable** — add it to your phone or laptop home screen like a real app.

## Getting started

1. Open the live link above.
2. On your phone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone/Safari) or the ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen (Android/Chrome).
3. On desktop: use your browser's Install app option, or just bookmark it.

Your data is saved locally in your own browser, so it stays private to your device.
